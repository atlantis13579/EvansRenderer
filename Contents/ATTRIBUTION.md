# Asset attribution

The code in Evans is self-contained. The meshes and textures under `Contents`
are test content distributed under their own licenses.

## Utah Teapot

- Files: `Models/UtahTeapot/`
- Model: Utah Teapot, originally modeled by Martin Newell (1975)
- This mesh revision: Kenzie Lamar, Vicarious Visions
- Source: [McGuire Computer Graphics Archive](https://casual-effects.com/g3d/data10/common/model/teapot/)
- License: CC0 / public domain dedication

This is a modified polygonal Utah Teapot, rather than Newell's original Bezier
patch data. The archive's white material and checkerboard UV inspection texture
are preserved. Historical shape reference: the [University of Utah's official
teapot versions](https://graphics.cs.utah.edu/teapot/), including Frank Crow's
widely used 1987 version. The historical model does not prescribe a material.

## Crytek Sponza Atrium

- Files: `Scenes/Sponza/`
- Model: Frank Meinl, Crytek (2010), inspired by Marko Dabrovic's Sponza
- Archive conversion and corrections: Morgan McGuire
- Canonical source: [McGuire Computer Graphics Archive](https://casual-effects.com/g3d/data10/common/model/crytek_sponza/)
- Download mirror: [MatthewBieda/VKEngine, commit d1f485b](https://github.com/MatthewBieda/VKEngine/tree/d1f485b9d02253a8db81577d7d9ae626422833fb/Models/Sponza)
- Missing opacity masks restored from [McNopper/OpenGL, commit b5a0247](https://github.com/McNopper/OpenGL/tree/b5a0247536ddad953556bc0f4ab29170317d3aa5/Binaries/sponza)
- The alternate material library in `Scenes/Sponza/PBR/` uses Alexandre Pestana's PBR textures as converted by Hans-Kristian Arntzen for the [Khronos Sponza glTF sample](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/f0920f5b32d172a30efe3f9257e965918831e6ea/Models/Sponza). Base color and normal images retain their source resolution. The linear surface maps combine base-color alpha coverage in red with the original roughness in green and metallic in blue. The upstream [license notice](Scenes/Sponza/PBR/LicenseRef-CRYENGINE-Agreement.txt) accompanies this set.
- License declared by the archive and mirror: [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)

Retain the CC BY 3.0 attribution with the original OBJ archive and the
upstream CRYENGINE license notice with the Khronos material set.
`Baked/` and `Baked/PBR/` contain the indirect lighting for their respective
material sets and the corresponding UV2 receiver streams.

## Stanford Dragon

- Files: `Models/StanfordDragon/`
- Original scan: Stanford University Computer Graphics Laboratory; Brian Curless and Marc Levoy, *A Volumetric Method for Building Complex Models from Range Images* (SIGGRAPH 1996).
- Original dataset and terms: [Stanford 3D Scanning Repository](https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/).
- Downloaded variant: [Sebastian Lague's Dragon_80K.obj](https://github.com/SebLague/Ray-Tracing/blob/e8981eae5e4bc392070a6fdbc7e1a74e295c86fa/Assets/Graphics/Dragon_80K.obj), used in his [Coding Adventure ray-tracing series](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLFt_AvWsXl0dlgwe4JQ0oZuleqOTjmox3).
- Source revision: `e8981eae5e4bc392070a6fdbc7e1a74e295c86fa`.
- Download SHA-256: `1cb230b0879f192eee74a887a062da2e4feb7047f2935bf65ddcaa151171de2f`.
- Geometry: 43,457 positions and 87,130 triangles, with the original authored corner normals. Only the material-library reference and explicit material assignment were changed; `dragon.mtl` is a local neutral material.
- Sebastian Lague's repository license is retained in `SebastianLague-LICENSE.txt`. The original Stanford model terms also apply: research use and free redistribution with attribution are permitted; commercial use requires Stanford's permission. The repository's software license does not replace the original model terms.

The demo uses rigid placements and optical material changes. Its reference floor
and backdrop are generated by Evans. Retain these credits with redistributed content.
